## Root-cause decision and alternatives

The current typed Choice experiment builder and runtime use `[context, option, context[i]*option[i]]`. If context-active and candidate-active are copied identically into both options, their option-specific effects cancel. Candidate yes/no one-hot coordinates do not overlap context task-kind `choice`, so diagonal products cannot express the necessary active-context × candidate-kind term. The four-quadrant test supports this exact representability limitation. A bounded cross architecture is therefore justified.

The M013 attempted quality comparison does not establish that the cross architecture improves predictive quality. Its cross head and legacy head both selected correctly on 0.50 of answerable M013 examples; this result is invalid because the one-factor and OOD split requirements were violated. Binary's near-zero Brier error on the reported ID cases also cannot explain or repair the M011 OOD regression. Binary nuisance sensitivity, deterministic order effects, and calibration remain open hypotheses. No regularization, architecture, or threshold was selected from M013 output.
